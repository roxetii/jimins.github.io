2D Games

Labyrinth Subliminal (2025)


I regard Labyrinth Subliminal as my magnum opus. It is a visual novel made on Ren'Py about memory and myth. I remember getting deeply impacted by Goethe's Faust and Nietzsche's The Birth of Tragedy, which gave me inspiration for the game's themes. I actually prefer to call this a "digital interactive experience" rather than a "game" because the mechanics are about cause and consequence (some consequences perhaps being more preferable to the player than others), not winning or losing.

Note: The game is posted on my public-facing itch account instead of my personal one.

Puff's Computer Quiz (2025)


Puff's computer quiz is a project I made for my computer studies class, where I used the Unity game engine to make a cute story-based quiz game featuring Puff, a puppy character. I tried to experiment with literary elements (I see games as a form of new storytelling medium) by adding plot twists—even some that are unnerving! I used Fungus to help me with the dialogue system, and I got to see how C# works.

Enerverse (2024)


Enerverse is a top down 2D RPG that was a contest entry for Energy Creates 2024. You get to play as Millie, a curious girl who can talk with animals! She gets to learn about the Canadian energy sector by exploring the world and chatting with whoever she finds.
